FIRST, GET TO KNOW THEM

Not all rays are
the same.

Rays are cartilaginous fish — their skeletons are made of flexible cartilage rather than bone. Most have flattened bodies and enlarged pectoral fins that help them glide through the water.

The ray family includes manta rays, stingrays, eagle rays and electric rays. Some filter tiny plankton from the water, while others search the seafloor for shellfish or use electric organs to stun prey.

Sharks' close relatives

Rays and sharks belong to the same group of cartilaginous fishes, known as elasmobranchs.
